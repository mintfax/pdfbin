§AWhy you might be here

  • typo in the ID
    IDs are lowercase, hyphen-separated, and always end in .pdf. Common slips: _ instead of -, plural where singular is canonical, or a trailing /. Browse the catalog to find the exact ID.
  • deprecated fixture
    Deprecated PDFs stay reachable at their original URL forever per the URL-immutability rule. A 404 here means the ID never existed or you've hit a typo - check /catalog.json for the canonical record.
  • never existed
    Plenty of plausible test PDFs aren't in the catalog yet. The catalog currently holds {{ $catalog.fixture_count }} PDFs; if you need a particular real-world document shape or corruption pattern that isn't covered, the catalog is open to additions in future releases.
